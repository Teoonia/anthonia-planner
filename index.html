        How it moves

        Tap Something came up to block out time. Blocks after it shift down. Use ↑ ↓ to swap order, Skip to drop a block for today, or Tomorrow to carry it over. You get 2 changes a day, then the plan locks. Real surprises go through Something came up, which does not count. A short break follows each work block, and a longer one follows long blocks. Each block takes the earliest free slot around your classes.
